<p>MoodyRoutine brings contextual automation, situational profiles, and event-driven workflows to any Android device running Android 8.0 (API 26) or higher. It reproduces the design philosophy, ergonomics, and automation capabilities of Samsung's One UI Modes & Routines without requiring root privileges or manufacturer-locked frameworks.</p>

<p><b>Modes (Situational Profiles)</b><br/>
Bundle device settings into situational profiles:</p>
<ul>
  <li><b>Sleep Mode:</b> Enables Do Not Disturb, reduces screen brightness, activates dark mode, and silences notifications.</li>
  <li><b>Work Mode:</b> Switches ringer to vibrate, mutes media, and restricts distracting applications.</li>
  <li><b>Driving Mode:</b> Enables auto-rotate, adjusts media volume, and launches navigation.</li>
  <li><b>Exercise Mode:</b> Configures media volume and launches workout tracking.</li>
  <li><b>Relax Mode:</b> Reduces screen brightness, silences alerts, and starts music or ambient audio.</li>
  <li><b>Custom Modes:</b> Define arbitrary modes with custom naming, color schemes, and 30+ categorized icons.</li>
  <li><b>Stay Focused (App Blocker):</b> Restricts selected applications while a mode is active, with options to close, snooze for 5 minutes, or turn off the mode.</li>
  <li><b>State Reversion:</b> Automatically snapshots prior volume, ringer, and display settings before mode activation, restoring them upon deactivation.</li>
  <li><b>Status Bar Notifications:</b> Ongoing foreground notification displaying active mode state with an instant "Turn off" action.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Routines (IF &rarr; THEN Automation)</b><br/>
Automate actions based on environmental triggers:</p>
<ul>
  <li><b>Conditions (IF):</b> Schedule (time ranges, recurrence), Battery &amp; Charging thresholds, Wi-Fi connected/disconnected (with SSID targeting), Bluetooth devices, App open/close, Headphones connected/disconnected, and Location geofencing via OpenStreetMap.</li>
  <li><b>Actions (THEN):</b> Audio sliders (Ring, media, alarm, notification), Ringer modes (Normal, vibrate, silent), Do Not Disturb states, Brightness adjustment, Auto-rotate toggle, Dark mode toggle, Home/Lock screen wallpapers, App launcher, and App shortcuts.</li>
</ul>
